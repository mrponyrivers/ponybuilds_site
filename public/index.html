No. Every pack shows each step on screen. If you can copy, paste and follow along, you can build it.

No. You pay once for each pack and keep it forever, including any updates I make to it.

A laptop and free accounts on GitHub and Railway. If a pack uses paid AI tools, the pack page lists the costs first.

Yes. Change the name, the look and the prices, and launch it as yours.
